EmberSpotter

A harmonized burning-activity calendar that joins the long MODIS hotspot record (2001 →) with the more detailed VIIRS NOAA-20 record (Apr 2018 →) in one consistent monthly series for any area of the world, for land and forest managers planning burn seasons and prevention.

Demo data. Every number, map cell and model metric on this page is synthetic and generated in your browser with a fixed random seed. The data layout follows the real active-fire attributes so the screens can be validated, but nothing here describes real fires.
How to use
  • Choose an area of interest (a country or one of its states or provinces, a rectangle, a polygon or lat/lon + radius) and a date range.
  • All charts use one harmonized series: estimated from MODIS before Apr 2018 and observed VIIRS NOAA-20 after. The map shows a harmonized activity grid; raw sensor counts appear only as reference (tooltips and Time series → Raw sources).
  • Read the burn calendar and check the time series before and after harmonization.

Before drawing conclusions, open Read before interpreting with the i button at the bottom-left of the map: it explains what a hotspot is, gaps from clouds, and how much to trust the harmonized values for your area.

Planned data source for the real build: NASA FIRMS active fire data (MODIS Collection 6.1 standard product and VIIRS NOAA-20 375 m).
